# Income-tax rules for a salaried resident individual: FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)

Scope: salary income only. Clause IDs in [brackets] are the citations an answer must use.
All amounts are in rupees per year.

## 1. Income under the head Salaries

[S17] Gross salary = basic salary + dearness allowance + house rent allowance received + all other taxable salary components.

[S10-13A] House rent allowance exemption. OLD regime only; not available in the new regime [S115BAC-2].
Exemption = the least of:
(a) HRA actually received;
(b) rent actually paid minus 10% of salary;
(c) 50% of salary if the residence is in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai; 40% of salary elsewhere.
"Salary" here means basic salary + dearness allowance only. If rent paid is less than 10% of salary, or no rent is paid, the exemption is nil.

[S16-ia] Standard deduction. Deduct from salary (after the HRA exemption) the lesser of the amount of that salary and:
- OLD regime: Rs 50,000.
- NEW regime: Rs 75,000.

## 2. Income from house property

[S24-b] Interest on a loan for a self-occupied house. OLD regime only: the deduction is the lesser of interest paid and Rs 2,00,000 (loan taken on or after 1 April 1999 for purchase or construction).
The resulting loss is set off against salary income under [S71-3A]. Gross total income cannot go below zero.
NEW regime: no deduction for a self-occupied house [S115BAC-2].

## 3. Deductions under Chapter VI-A (OLD regime only)

[S80C] Investments and payments eligible under section 80C: deduction up to Rs 1,50,000.
[S80CCD-1B] Own contribution to the National Pension System: further deduction up to Rs 50,000, over and above [S80C].
[S80D-SELF] Health insurance for self and family: up to Rs 25,000, or Rs 50,000 if the taxpayer is a senior citizen (age 60 or more).
[S80D-PARENTS] Health insurance for parents: up to Rs 25,000, or Rs 50,000 if a parent is a senior citizen. This limit is separate from [S80D-SELF].
[S80A-2] Total Chapter VI-A deductions cannot exceed gross total income.
[S115BAC-2] NEW regime: none of [S10-13A], [S24-b], [S80C], [S80CCD-1B], [S80D-SELF], [S80D-PARENTS] is allowed.

## 4. Total income

Gross total income = income under Salaries minus the [S24-b] loss (not below zero).
Total income = gross total income minus Chapter VI-A deductions.
[S288A] Round total income to the nearest multiple of Rs 10: first drop any paise; then if the last digit is 5 or more round up, otherwise round down. Use this rounded figure for every later step.

## 5. Tax rates

NEW regime (default) on total income:
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S1] up to Rs 3,00,000: nil
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S2] Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 7,00,000: 5%
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S3] Rs 7,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 10%
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S4] Rs 10,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 15%
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S5] Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 15,00,000: 20%
[S115BAC-1A-ii-S6] above Rs 15,00,000: 30%

OLD regime, age below 60:
[FS3-A-i-S1] up to Rs 2,50,000: nil
[FS3-A-i-S2] Rs 2,50,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
[FS3-A-i-S3] Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
[FS3-A-i-S4] above Rs 10,00,000: 30%

OLD regime, age 60 or more but less than 80 at any time during the year:
[FS3-A-ii-S1] up to Rs 3,00,000: nil
[FS3-A-ii-S2] Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
[FS3-A-ii-S3] Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
[FS3-A-ii-S4] above Rs 10,00,000: 30%

OLD regime, age 80 or more at any time during the year:
[FS3-A-iii-S1] up to Rs 5,00,000: nil
[FS3-A-iii-S2] Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
[FS3-A-iii-S3] above Rs 10,00,000: 30%

## 6. Rebate under section 87A (resident individual, applied to tax before surcharge and cess)

[S87A-OLD] OLD regime: if total income does not exceed Rs 5,00,000, rebate = the lesser of the tax and Rs 12,500. There is no marginal relief above Rs 5,00,000.
[S87A-NEW-a] NEW regime: if total income does not exceed Rs 7,00,000, rebate = the lesser of the tax and Rs 25,000.
[S87A-NEW-b] NEW regime marginal relief: if total income exceeds Rs 7,00,000 and the tax exceeds (total income minus Rs 7,00,000), rebate = tax minus (total income minus Rs 7,00,000).

## 7. Surcharge (on tax after rebate)

Rate by total income:
[SURCH-50L] above Rs 50,00,000 up to Rs 1,00,00,000: 10%
[SURCH-1CR] above Rs 1,00,00,000 up to Rs 2,00,00,000: 15%
[SURCH-2CR] above Rs 2,00,00,000 up to Rs 5,00,00,000: 25%
[SURCH-5CR-OLD] OLD regime above Rs 5,00,00,000: 37%
[SURCH-5CR-NEW] NEW regime above Rs 5,00,00,000: 25% (the 37% rate does not apply)

[SURCH-MR] Marginal relief. Let T be the lower limit of the income band the taxpayer falls in (Rs 50 lakh, Rs 1 crore, Rs 2 crore or Rs 5 crore). Tax plus surcharge must not exceed: (tax plus surcharge on a total income of exactly T, under the same regime and age band) + (total income minus T). Reduce the surcharge by any excess. No marginal relief applies to cess.

## 8. Cess and final rounding

[CESS] Health and Education Cess: 4% of (tax after rebate + surcharge).
[S288B] Round the final tax payable to the nearest multiple of Rs 10: first drop any paise; then if the last digit is 5 or more round up, otherwise round down.

## 9. Sequence

Salary income -> house property loss -> Chapter VI-A -> [S288A] -> slab tax -> rebate -> surcharge with marginal relief -> cess -> [S288B]. Compute both regimes separately.

[S115BAC-1A] Regime choice: recommend the regime with the lower tax payable. If they are equal, recommend the new regime, which is the default.

Sources: Finance (No. 2) Bill 2024 and its Memorandum (indiabudget.gov.in/budget2024-25), Income Tax Department help pages and guidance (incometax.gov.in, incometaxindia.gov.in).
